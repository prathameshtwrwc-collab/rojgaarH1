## Writing Style

- **Concise.** Lines are short. No marketing fluff.
- **Confident.** Direct statements, not hedging.
- **Human.** Conversational without being casual.
- **Employment-focused.** Every sentence relates to work, careers, hiring, or business.
- **Plain language.** No corporate jargon, no "synergy", no "leverage".
- **Action-oriented.** Buttons are short imperatives.
- **Concrete numbers.** Stats are exact.
- **Hindi-friendly brand.** "Rojgaar" means "employment" in Hindi. The English copy is the official surface.

---

## Tone Rules

- Avoid superlatives ("best", "world's leading", "top-tier") unless they are part of an existing UI string.
- Avoid emoji in product copy.
- Avoid exclamation points in body copy. The hero `Hai!` is a brand-mark exception.
- Do not add taglines. The hero paragraph already serves that role.
- Use sentence case. Do not write headings in ALL CAPS. The eyebrow uses uppercase because it is a typographic label, not a body sentence.

---

## Locked Content Decisions

- The brand name is `Rojgaar Hai`. Do not translate it. Do not change the casing.
- The hero CTA pair is `I am a Job Seeker` and `I am an Employer`. They appear twice in the site (hero and final CTA).
- Statistics are `50K+`, `5K+`, `20K+`, `95%`. Do not round them. Do not change them.
- The testimonial quote is final. The word `easier` is the only word with a hand-drawn annotation.
- The footer has exactly five nav links. No newsletter. No copyright line.
- The footer has exactly three social icons. No more.
